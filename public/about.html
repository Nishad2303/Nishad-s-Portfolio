01 MECHANICAL ENGINEER · AEROSPACE

ABOUT ME

I’m Nishad Hooli, a Mechanical Engineer driven by a passion for aerospace, structural engineering, and the design of high-performance mechanical systems.

Currently working as a Structural Repair Engineer – Design, specializing in CRJ aircraft, I contribute to aircraft structural repair design, bringing engineering principles into the demanding world of aviation. My journey also includes an internship at ISRO–LEOS, where I worked on a precision pneumatic system for aerospace-grade mirror testing.

Previously, as Team Captain of aeroKLE, I led the design and development of UAVs and RC aircraft, securing national rankings including AIR 1 & 2 at Aero Design Challenge and AIR 3 at SAE India Aerothon.

My technical interests span structural design, CAD modeling, FEA, design optimization, and prototyping, with hands-on experience in SolidWorks, Siemens NX, ANSYS, and MATLAB. My work includes a 3D-printed waste recycling machine, an electromagnetic payload gripper, and published research on UAV landing strut optimization.

I believe great engineering goes beyond creating designs — it’s about understanding how systems behave, solving real-world challenges, and building solutions that perform when precision matters most.
